COM CM731 • Human Centered Design I

Taught byProf. Jen Jiun Choi

Human-Centered Design I introduces students to the end-to-end design process through a human-centered and strategic lens. Students learn to conduct user research, uncover insights, frame problems, and generate solutions, emphasizing empathy, inclusivity, and holistic thinking. The course focuses on building a strong core knowledge in design frameworks, storytelling, and critical analysis, preparing students from diverse backgrounds to approach UX/UI, research, and product challenges thoughtfully. HCD I equips students to think strategically, not just execute, providing the skills and mindset needed for more advanced work in HCD II and future design leadership roles.

Syllabus Class Drive Figma Office Hours

Product Design Core

Apply an iterative digital product design process from strategy, research and ideation through prototyping, validation, and testing.

Ideation & Problem Solving

Use structured creative methods to generate, refine, and evaluate design solutions.

Research & Validation

Conduct user research and usability testing to identify and validate user needs and pain points.

Strategic & Accessible Design

Integrate strategic thinking, creativity, and accessibility to develop inclusive and scalable digital products.

Communication & Storytelling

Clearly present design concepts, decisions, and validated outcomes through visual and verbal storytelling.

Human-Centered Thinking

Approach design challenges thoughtfully through empathy, inclusivity, and holistic thinking.
